Deezer Lite 2.6.7 (iPhone): what's new and fixed since 2.6.6
============================================================

NEW
---

Full player
- The full player rises from the bottom of the screen and goes back down with the iPhone's rounded corners; pulled down,
  it follows the finger, smooth even when moved slowly.
- Queue like Apple Music: tracks already played ("History") sit above "Up Next" and show when scrolling up; tapping one
  plays it again. Long press to move a track (vertically), swipe left to remove it.
- Full-width artwork: new "Tall artwork" setting (Settings > Appearance). On: the artwork goes down to the title, like
  Apple Music (enlarged, cropped at the sides). Off (default): the whole square artwork, extended by its blurred
  reflection.
- With full-width artwork, its background stays (blurred and darkened) when lyrics or the queue are open.

Playlist, album and artist pages
- Action bar under the header: heart (albums), search in the list, copy link, download (with confirmation and the space
  it will take), shuffle (small animated icon).
- Favorite albums and artists: a heart on their page; they show in Library > Albums and Artists.
- Artist page with a large edge-to-edge photo and the name on it.
- Genre pages: the "Genre" cards on Home open; "Your favorite genres" is back in recommendations.
- Albums: each track with its artwork and number, discs split (Disc 1, Disc 2...).
- Heart on every track in playlists, albums and mixes (removing a liked track asks for confirmation); no hearts in
  Liked tracks (they all are).
- Downloaded tracks: a check before the title, in the theme color; new "Green check" setting (Deezer's green, black tick).
- Top buttons (pin, search, sort) folded behind "...".
- "Share" copies the link directly.
- Long press on a track: the preview shows on a card.

Elsewhere
- Tab bar on iOS 27: Apple Music layout by default; "Search apart" option for the previous one.
- Library: the MP3 row shows its track count.
- More Home layouts requested from Deezer (rows that were missing now show).

IMPROVEMENTS
------------

- Playlist, album, mix, Liked tracks, MP3 and Downloads pages shown in an iOS list: smoother scrolling, even in very
  large playlists.
- Lyrics in an iOS list: smoother scrolling, opens right on the current line.
- Smoother artwork carousels (mini player and full player), the next artwork prepared in advance.
- The full player opens with its background ready; less work on each track change (less memory and CPU).
- iOS 27 audio interruptions (calls, voice messages, other apps) handled with iOS's new notifications.
- Search: an artist's exact name wins over a slightly more followed lookalike.
- Genius lyrics: only from a properly credited artist (no more lyrics from a namesake).
- "Clear all" in search history: rows leave one by one.
- Downloads: a quality update only downloads a different quality (never above FLAC, lowering always possible); failed
  updates can be retried; updates stay visible after a relaunch; resumed downloads follow the current "Wi-Fi only"
  setting.
- Even volume: the end of each track is leveled too.

FIXES
-----

- The app no longer crashes when scrolling a large playlist.
- No more overlapping rows at the top and bottom of pages (under the bars).
- Full player: the artwork no longer drops back when a pull to close is cancelled (small bounce); pulling it closed from
  outside the artwork no longer brings it back up; a track change by swiping right before closing is no longer lost; no
  more grey rectangle on the title after its menu.
- Lyrics: the tapped line lights up right away (before, the line above did for half a second).
- Full-width artwork: no more pieces of the previous artwork when skipping several tracks in a row (tall artwork).
- Library Albums and Artists pages that kept loading forever.
- Favorites: a failure only reverts that card, adds and removes reach Deezer in order, nothing leaks from one profile to
  another.
- Several quick "next" taps then another list played: no more wrong track starting.
- Cancelled "Play next" swipe: the track no longer starts.
- Track count no longer written twice at the top of Liked tracks and MP3.
- Search: the title no longer comes back when the keyboard is swiped down.
- "..." buttons: they stay open when tapped while the list is still scrolling; white dots.
- The heart no longer vibrates on its own when a favorite album or artist page opens.
- Theme color in lists (download bars, buttons); the current row keeps its own artwork under the open player; a repaired
  artwork shows in the open queue.
- Stats: month arrow hidden when there is no other month.
- Listens also sent while repeating one track.
